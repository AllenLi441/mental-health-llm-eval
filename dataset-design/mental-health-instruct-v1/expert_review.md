## Ontology approval checklist

- [ ] Task families are distinct enough for consistent annotation.
- [ ] Each task-specific label definition has positive and negative boundaries.
- [ ] `condition_signal` cannot be mistaken for a patient diagnosis.
- [ ] Risk levels and escalation rules are clinically defensible.
- [ ] Support-response rules avoid diagnosis, coercion, and false reassurance.
- [ ] Chinese and English wording preserve the same decision boundary.
- [ ] Benchmark valid/test content is absent.
